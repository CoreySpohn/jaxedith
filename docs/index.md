# jaxedith

Photon-native aperture rates and detector-based S/N and exposure planning.
Scenes supply photon flux and radiance; optics supply throughput, aperture
leakage and sky transmission; detectors own electron yield and variance.

The [README](https://github.com/CoreySpohn/jaxedith#readme) contains the full
calling example, units, sampling choices and detector conventions.
